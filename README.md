## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Aegis Force: The Scorian War**. This trainer provides optional assistance during exploration and tactical battles. It can protect party members, improve combat efficiency, simplify progression, and reduce resource management while preserving the game's grid-based strategy, character abilities, recruitable party members, bond attacks, inventory, and equipment systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experience the story and exploration with less grinding, test party compositions and abilities, experiment with tactical encounters, or replay battles with reduced resource pressure.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after entering the explorable world or a tactical battle. Movement assistance applies to controllable party members on the combat grid and is intended for testing positioning, flanking routes, and difficult objectives. Disable the option before ending a turn if a battle behaves unexpectedly.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the inventory screen before activating inventory-related options. Use item protection for consumables and other supported items, then disable it before making major equipment changes. Save the game after completing purchases, crafting, or upgrades.

### World & Environment

Aegis Force features full world and town exploration across the continent of Ianua, with locations, secrets, recruitable characters, items, and story events to discover. Progression and inventory options can reduce backtracking while leaving exploration, dialogue, cutscenes, and recruitment available through normal play.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate combat options after the tactical grid appears and before selecting an action. Health, resource, movement, damage, bond attack, and victory features support the game's turn-based battles, which use character abilities, spells, attacks, positioning, and coordinated party tactics. Disable instant victory when you want rewards, progression, and battle outcomes to develop normally.
